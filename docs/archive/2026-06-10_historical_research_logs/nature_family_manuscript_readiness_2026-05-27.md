## Current paper structure

### Title direction

Working title:

> Sparse release observations reveal mechanistic states for calibrated
> drug-release forecasting

Avoid:

- foundation model
- world model
- universal drug-release AI
- active learning as point-prediction champion

### Results order

1. Early release observations contain the dominant predictive information,
   while classical sparse extrapolators fail under future-only evaluation.
2. Release curves collapse onto low-dimensional, regime-dependent feasible
   kinetic states.
3. The theta/ODE middle layer yields statistically positive gain over direct
   Q(t) regression.
4. Raw FIB-CASP under-covers; split-conformal recalibration restores
   near-nominal pointwise random-split coverage with explicit width cost.
5. Active observer provides a decision/UQ module, not the strongest point
   predictor.
6. Liposome shows decoder extensibility; chitosan decides whether the story
   becomes prospective and wet-lab validated.
